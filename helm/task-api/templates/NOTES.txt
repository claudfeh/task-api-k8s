The task-api chart is installed.

Reach the app:
  kubectl port-forward svc/task-api 5000:5000
  then open http://localhost:5000/health in your browser.

Or through the Ingress (fake the hostname, skipping DNS setup):
  kubectl port-forward -n ingress-nginx svc/ingress-nginx-controller 8080:80
  curl.exe -H "Host: task-api.local" http://localhost:8080/api/tasks

Try changing configuration without touching YAML:
  helm upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} . --set replicaCount=3
  kubectl get pods   # watch a third app pod appear

Remove everything Helm installed:
  helm uninstall {{ .Release.Name }}
